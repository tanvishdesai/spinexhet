# Literature List

1. Krishna et al., "The Disagreement Problem in Explainable Machine Learning: A Practitioner's Perspective", 2022. Direct predecessor formalizing explanation disagreement.
2. Hedström et al., "Navigating the Maze of Explainable AI: A Systematic Approach to Evaluating Methods and Metrics", NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks 2024. Large-scale XAI benchmark to position against.
3. Adebayo et al., "Sanity Checks for Saliency Maps", NeurIPS 2018. Methodological foundation for validating saliency reliability.
4. Selvaraju et al., "Grad-CAM", ICCV 2017. CAM method used in the benchmark.
5. Chattopadhay et al., "Grad-CAM++", WACV 2018. CAM variant used in the benchmark.
6. Sundararajan et al., "Axiomatic Attribution for Deep Networks", ICML 2017. Integrated Gradients foundation.
7. Zeiler and Fergus, "Visualizing and Understanding Convolutional Networks", ECCV 2014. Occlusion/visualization foundation.
8. Abnar and Zuidema, "Quantifying Attention Flow in Transformers", ACL 2020. Attention Rollout foundation.
9. Koh et al., "Concept Bottleneck Models", ICML 2020. Ante-hoc concept model foundation.
10. Ghassemi et al., "The False Hope of Current Approaches to Explainable AI in Health Care", Lancet Digital Health 2021. Clinical XAI caution.
11. Dosovitskiy et al., "An Image is Worth 16x16 Words", ICLR 2021. ViT foundation.
12. Touvron et al., "Training Data-Efficient Image Transformers and Distillation through Attention", ICML 2021. DeiT foundation.
13. Liu et al., "A ConvNet for the 2020s", CVPR 2022. ConvNeXt foundation.
14. Tan and Le, "EfficientNet", ICML 2019. EfficientNet foundation.
15. Wah et al., "The Caltech-UCSD Birds-200-2011 Dataset", 2011 technical report. CUB dataset citation.
